# INC-AMX5200-021: Particle excursion traced to chamber wall polymer flaking

**Date logged:** synthetic record
**Reported by:** Equipment Technician (synthetic)
**Severity:** Medium — 1 lot held, no scrap

## Symptom
Post-etch particle adder count on the seasoning monitor wafer spiked to 40+
adders (>0.16 µm), well above the 15-adder release threshold, despite a
recent wet clean and normal RF match performance.

## Investigation
Chamber viewport inspection initially showed buildup below the Level 2
threshold defined in SOP-AMX5200-001, so a wet-clean request was not
triggered by the standard visual check. Closer inspection with a borescope
found polymer buildup concentrated on the chamber wall directly opposite the
gas injector, an area not fully visible through the standard viewport angle.
Fluorocarbon-rich recipes run in the prior week had built up polymer
preferentially at that location, and flaking from that specific spot was the
particle source.

## Resolution
Performed an out-of-cycle wet clean focused on the wall area opposite the
injector. Particle count returned to baseline (<10 adders).

## Lesson learned
The standard viewport visual check can under-report wall buildup that
accumulates opposite the gas injector, because that area is partially
occluded from the normal viewing angle. After any week with heavy
fluorocarbon recipe usage, a borescope check of the injector-opposite wall
area is more reliable than the standard viewport check alone, even if the
viewport reading looks acceptable.
