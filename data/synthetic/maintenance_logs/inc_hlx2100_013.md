# INC-HLX2100-013: Reticle-specific CD excursion from pod contamination

**Date logged:** synthetic record
**Reported by:** Litho Technician (synthetic)
**Severity:** Medium — 1 lot with CD excursion, caught at metrology

## Symptom
One specific reticle produced a CD excursion on two separate lots, roughly a
week apart, while all other reticles running the same layer on the same
scanner were within spec.

## Investigation
Because the issue was reticle-specific rather than tool-wide, the
investigation focused on the reticle and its pod rather than the scanner.
Reticle pod inspection found particle contamination on the pellicle-side of
the pod, consistent with SOP-HLX2100-004's note that pod contamination is the
leading cause of hard-to-reproduce, reticle-specific excursions.

## Resolution
Reticle was cleaned and re-pelliclized. Pod was replaced. Subsequent lots on
the same reticle were within spec.

## Lesson learned
When an excursion is isolated to a single reticle rather than appearing
tool-wide, escalate to reticle pod inspection early rather than investigating
the scanner itself — this saved significant time on the second occurrence
because the pattern from the first incident was recognized quickly.
