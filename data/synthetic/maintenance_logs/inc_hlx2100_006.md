# INC-HLX2100-006: Overlay excursion misdiagnosed as lens fault, actually stage thermal drift

**Date logged:** synthetic record
**Reported by:** Litho Equipment Engineer (synthetic)
**Severity:** High — 4 hours of downtime, 1 lot held

## Symptom
Overlay calibration failed at start of shift, error at +4.5nm (spec ±3nm).
The team's first assumption was a lens alignment fault, since the overlay
excursion pattern resembled prior lens-related events, and lens realignment
was scheduled.

## Investigation
Before lens realignment began, a check of wafer stage temperature stability
logs (per SOP-HLX2100-004 Section 2) showed a 0.4°C drift in the stage chuck
temperature over the preceding 6 hours, coinciding with a facility chiller
setpoint change made the previous night for an unrelated reason. The overlay
signature from stage thermal drift is very similar to a lens alignment
issue at this magnitude, which is why the team's first assumption was wrong.

## Resolution
Facility chiller setpoint was corrected. Stage temperature stabilized within
2 hours. Overlay calibration passed without any lens intervention.

## Lesson learned
Stage thermal drift and lens alignment faults produce nearly identical
overlay error signatures at moderate magnitudes. Checking stage temperature
stability logs first, before scheduling a lens realignment, avoids
unnecessary tool downtime — lens realignment takes significantly longer than
a chiller setpoint correction and was avoidable here.
