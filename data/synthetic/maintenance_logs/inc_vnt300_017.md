# INC-VNT300-017: Single degraded heater zone masked by control thermocouple

**Date logged:** synthetic record
**Reported by:** Shift Engineer (synthetic)
**Severity:** Medium — repeated uniformity fails after passing showerhead/gap checks

## Symptom
Film uniformity failed on three consecutive monitor wafers with a consistent
edge-high thickness signature. Showerhead inspection and electrode gap
verification both passed per SOP-VNT300-002.

## Investigation
The primary control thermocouple for the outer heater zone read within 1°C of
setpoint throughout. A secondary probe check (per SOP-VNT300-002 Section 5)
found the physical wafer-surface temperature in that zone running
approximately 6°C below the control thermocouple reading, indicating the
heater element itself had degraded even though the thermocouple used for
closed-loop control was reading correctly.

## Resolution
Replaced the outer zone heater element. Post-replacement secondary probe
check confirmed physical temperature matched control thermocouple reading
within 1°C. Uniformity returned to spec.

## Lesson learned
A heater zone can degrade in a way that the control thermocouple does not
detect, because the thermocouple and the heater element can fail
independently — this produces a uniformity signature that looks identical to
a showerhead or gap issue. The secondary probe check should not be treated as
optional or "only if the obvious checks fail twice" — on this tool family it
is a distinct failure mode that the primary control loop cannot see at all.
