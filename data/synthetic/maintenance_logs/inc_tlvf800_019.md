# INC-TLVF800-019: Elevated particles for multiple runs after cracked boat cleaning

**Date logged:** synthetic record
**Reported by:** Furnace Technician (synthetic)
**Severity:** Low — 2 runs with elevated particle counts, no scrap

## Symptom
Two consecutive runs on the same quartz boat showed elevated particle
adders on monitor wafers, though still within the release threshold. The
boat had a hairline crack noted during pre-load inspection but was cleaned
and judged acceptable for continued use per visual inspection.

## Investigation
The crack itself did not cause immediate particle failure, but the two runs
following the crack's discovery both trended higher than the boat's normal
particle baseline before returning to normal on the third run, consistent
with residual particle generation from the crack site continuing for a short
period after cleaning.

## Resolution
Boat was retired after the third run rather than continuing to monitor it
run-by-run.

## Lesson learned
A cracked boat that passes visual cleaning inspection can still elevate
particle counts for the next two to three runs, not just the immediate next
run — this is why SOP-TLVF800-005 flags elevated risk for "the following 2-3
runs even after visual cleaning" rather than treating a cleaned crack as
fully resolved.
