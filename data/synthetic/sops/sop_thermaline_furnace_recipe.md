# SOP-TLVF800-005: Recipe Qualification and Boat Loading Procedure

**Equipment:** ThermaLine VF-800 Vertical Diffusion Furnace
**Applies to:** Shift engineers, furnace technicians
**Revision:** 3.3

## 1. Purpose
Defines the boat loading, ramp qualification, and recipe verification
procedure for the ThermaLine VF-800 vertical furnace.

## 2. Boat Loading
1. Confirm boat slot map matches the lot traveler before loading; wafer
   spacing must match the qualified recipe boat map exactly.
2. Inspect quartz boat for visible particle buildup or hairline cracks before
   each load. A cracked boat can still complete a run successfully but
   increases particle risk on the following 2-3 runs even after visual
   cleaning.

## 3. Ramp and Soak Qualification
1. Verify ramp rate matches recipe specification; deviations greater than 2%
   should be flagged even if the furnace reaches final soak temperature within
   tolerance, since ramp-rate deviation affects film properties independent of
   final temperature.
2. Confirm all temperature zones reach soak setpoint within ±1°C before the
   process timer starts.

## 4. Common Non-Uniformity Root Causes
1. Top-to-bottom thickness non-uniformity that appears gradually over several
   weeks, rather than suddenly, is most often caused by slow drift in the gas
   injector flow distribution rather than a heater zone fault — check injector
   flow calibration before assuming a heater issue.
2. A sudden, single-run non-uniformity event is more likely a boat loading or
   particle issue than a furnace hardware fault.

## 5. Post-Run Verification
1. Measure thickness/sheet resistance on monitor wafers at top, middle, and
   bottom boat positions.
2. Confirm within-boat uniformity is within qualified spec before releasing
   the lot.

## 6. Sign-off
Record ramp qualification, zone temperatures, and post-run measurements in the
furnace log before lot disposition.
