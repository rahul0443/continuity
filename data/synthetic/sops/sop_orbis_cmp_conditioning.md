# SOP-ORB7-003: Pad Conditioning and Slurry Line Qualification

**Equipment:** Orbis CMP-7 Polisher (Cu/oxide CMP)
**Applies to:** Shift engineers, equipment technicians
**Revision:** 2.1

## 1. Purpose
Defines the pad conditioning schedule and slurry line qualification steps
required before releasing the Orbis CMP-7 for production polishing.

## 2. Pad Conditioning
1. Condition the polishing pad for 90 seconds at standard conditioning disk
   pressure prior to the first wafer of each shift ("ex-situ conditioning").
2. Inspect pad surface for glazing (a smooth, shiny appearance indicating
   reduced asperity). Glazed pads produce declining removal rate over the
   course of a shift even when conditioning time is nominally correct.
3. Pad life is tracked by wafer count, not just calendar time. Replace pad at
   the qualified wafer-count limit regardless of visual condition.

## 3. Slurry Line Qualification
1. Purge slurry supply lines for 5 minutes prior to first use each day to clear
   settled particles from static lines.
2. Verify slurry flow rate is within 5% of setpoint at the point-of-use sensor,
   not just at the supply tank — line restriction downstream of the tank sensor
   is not visible from the tank-side reading alone.
3. Confirm slurry pH and particle concentration against the daily QC certificate
   before use.

## 4. Removal Rate Verification
1. Run a blanket monitor wafer at standard recipe conditions.
2. Confirm removal rate is within ±7% of the target rate and within-wafer
   non-uniformity (1-sigma) is below 6%.
3. If removal rate drifts low despite passing pad and slurry checks, check
   retaining ring wear next — a worn retaining ring changes the pressure
   distribution at the wafer edge before it produces a visually obvious defect.

## 5. Post-CMP Clean Verification
1. Confirm post-CMP brush box scrub and rinse cycle completed without alarm.
2. Spot-check for residual slurry particles using the inline particle counter;
   escalate to equipment engineering if adder counts exceed the qualified
   threshold on two consecutive lots.

## 6. Sign-off
Log conditioning time, slurry qualification results, and removal rate
verification in the shift log before releasing the tool to production.
